# Dynamic Business Suite - Video Asset

Place your exported 16:9 MP4 video file here named:
`promo.mp4`

Path: `/videos/dynamic-business-suite/promo.mp4`

Once placed, the portfolio will automatically stream your video with custom HTML5 controls and thumbnail poster!
Alternatively, you can specify an external video URL (YouTube, Vimeo, Cloudflare Stream, Google Drive) in `src/data/projects.ts`.
